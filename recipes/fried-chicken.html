Fried Chicken

Description

This classic fried chicken features a shatteringly crisp, golden-brown crust wrapping around incredibly juicy, well-seasoned meat. A simple buttermilk marinade keeps the chicken tender and flavorful, while a spiced flour coating delivers the perfect crunch with every single bite.

Ingredients

    Chicken & Marinade

  • Buttermilk: 500 ml (tenders and moisturises the meat)
  • Chicken pieces: 1 kg of bone-in, skin-on chicken pieces (thighs, drumsticks, or breasts)
  • Hot sauce: 1 tablespoon (optional, for subtle depth)

    • Crispy Coating

  • Flour: 250g all-purpose flour
  • Cornstarch: 4 tablespoons (makes the crust extra crispy)
  • Spice blend: 1 tablespoon salt, 1 tablespoon garlic powder, 1 tablespoon paprika, 1 teaspoon black pepper, 1/2 teaspoon cayenne pepper

    • For Frying

  • Oil: Vegetable, peanut, or sunflower oil (enough to submerge the chicken halfway)

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Marinate: Whisk the buttermilk and hot sauce in a large bowl. Submerge the chicken pieces and refrigerate for at least 1 hour (or overnight for the best results).
  2. Mix Coating: In a shallow dish, thoroughly whisk together the flour, cornstarch, and the spice blend.
  3. Dredge: Remove a piece of chicken from the buttermilk, letting excess drip off. Coat it completely in the flour mixture, pressing firmly so the flour sticks. Shake off any excess. (Tip: Drizzle 2 tablespoons of buttermilk into the flour beforehand to create extra crunchy flakes).
  4. Heat Oil: Pour oil into a deep skillet or Dutch oven until it reaches about 2.5 cm deep. Heat the oil to 175°C (350°F) over medium-high heat.
  5. Fry: Carefully add the chicken to the hot oil in batches—do not overcrowd the pan. Fry drumsticks and thighs for 12–14 minutes, and breasts for 10–12 minutes, turning occasionally, until the crust is golden brown and the internal temperature hits 75°C (165°F).
  6. Drain & Rest: Remove the chicken and let it drain on a wire cooling rack placed over a baking sheet for 5 minutes. This keeps the bottom from getting soggy.
