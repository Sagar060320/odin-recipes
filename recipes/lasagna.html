Lasagna

Description

This ultimate homemade lasagna is the definition of comfort food, featuring layers of tender pasta, a rich, slow-simmered meat sauce, and a velvety three-cheese blend. Every bite delivers a perfect balance of savory herbs, tangy tomatoes, and melted goodness, making it an instant crowd-pleaser for family dinners or casual gatherings. Baked until golden and bubbly, it fills your kitchen with an irresistible aroma that calls everyone to the table.

Ingredients

    Meat sauce

  • Ground beef & Italian sausage: 450g each (or use all beef)
  • Crushed tomatoes: 2 cans (approx. 800g total)
  • Tomato paste: 2 tablespoons
  • Onion & garlic: 1 finely chopped onion, 4 minced cloves
  • Seasoning: 1 tablespoon Italian seasoning, salt, and black pepper to taste

    • Cheese Mixture

  • Ricotta cheese: 450g (or use small-curd cottage cheese)
  • Egg: 1 large egg (helps bind the cheese)
  • Parmesan cheese: 50g grated
  • Parsley: 2 tablespoons fresh, chopped

    • Assembly

  • Lasagna noodles: 12 sheets (oven-ready or boiled)
  • Mozzarella cheese: 400g shredded

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Cook the Meat Sauce: Brown the beef, sausage, and onion in a large pot over medium heat. Drain excess fat. Add garlic, tomato paste, crushed tomatoes, and seasonings. Simmer on low heat for 20 minutes.
  2. Mix the Cheeses: In a medium bowl, stir together the ricotta, egg, Parmesan, and parsley until smooth.
  3. Prep the Oven: Preheat your oven to 190°C (375°F).
  4. Layer the Lasagna:
    1. Spread 1 cup of meat sauce at the bottom of a 9x13-inch baking dish.
    2. Place 4 noodles on top.
    3. Spread half of the ricotta mixture, followed by a third of the mozzarella and meat sauce.
    4. Repeat the layers (noodles, ricotta, mozzarella, sauce).
    5. Top with the final layer of noodles, remaining sauce, and the rest of the mozzarella.
  5. Bake: Cover loosely with foil and bake for 25 minutes. Remove the foil and bake for another 20 minutes until the cheese is bubbly and golden brown.
  6. Rest and Serve: Let the lasagna rest for 15 minutes before slicing so it holds its shape.
