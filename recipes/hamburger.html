Hamburger

Description

This classic homemade cheeseburger features a juicy, perfectly seared beef patty topped with melted cheese, crisp toppings, and a signature sauce inside a toasted brioche bun. It delivers the ultimate backyard barbecue flavor in less than 20 minutes, offering a perfect balance of savory meat, tangy condiments, and fresh, crisp crunch.

Ingredients

    Patties

  • Ground beef: 450g (80/20 lean-to-fat ratio for maximum juiciness)
  • Seasoning: 1 teaspoon salt, 1/2 teaspoon black pepper, 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder

    • Assembly & Toppings

  • Buns: 4 brioche or sesame seed burger buns
  • Cheese: 4 slices of American or Cheddar cheese
  • Fresh toppings: 4 lettuce leaves, 4 tomato slices, 8 pickle slices, 1/4 thinly sliced red onion
  • Burger sauce: 3 tablespoons mayonnaise, 1 tablespoon ketchup, 1 teaspoon relish

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Shape Patties: Divide the beef into 4 equal portions. Shape them into patties roughly 2 cm wider than your buns. Press a small indentation into the center of each patty with your thumb to prevent shrinking.
  2. Season: Season both sides of the patties generously with the salt, pepper, and garlic powder mix right before cooking.
  3. Cook: Heat a cast-iron skillet or grill to high heat. Sear the patties for 3 to 4 minutes on the first side until a deep crust forms.
  4. Flip and Cheese: Flip the patties and immediately place a slice of cheese on each. Cook for another 2 to 3 minutes until the cheese melts and the beef reaches your preferred doneness.
  5. Toast Buns: Butter the inside of the buns and toast them face-down on the hot pan or grill for 1 minute until golden brown.
  6. Assemble: Mix the sauce ingredients together. Spread the sauce on the bottom bun, layer with lettuce, tomato, the cheesy patty, pickles, and onions, then top with the remaining bun.
